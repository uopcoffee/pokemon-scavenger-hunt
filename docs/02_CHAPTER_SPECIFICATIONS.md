# 02. Chapter Specifications

> Release-order note: the approved V2 runtime runs Professor Oak's Water
> Research Lab as Chapter 3 at 10:15, followed by the Pokémon Center as
> Chapter 4. The canonical party-day clock is in `01_MASTER_RUN_OF_SHOW.md`
> and `10_PARTY_DAY_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Global chapter loop

Each numbered chapter follows this rhythm:

1. Story transmission
2. Destination or location reveal
3. Narrator teaser establishes the waiting character without showing their
   live dialogue
4. Physical challenge briefing or optional digital warmup
5. Phone Captain completes the named **open cue** hold
6. Privacy shield and private cast Quick Card
7. Performer delivers the live entrance and runs the real-world challenge
8. Phone Captain completes the protected Mission Complete hold
9. Return shield confirms Luca can see the screen
10. Reward reveal and inventory update
11. Code-fragment recording when applicable
12. Transition or cliffhanger

# Chapter 1: Trainer Orientation

## Location

Home Base, front porch or living room.

## Character

**Auntie Ariel: Pokémon League Recruiter and Master of Ceremonies**

Ariel should be highly theatrical, funny, and energetic. Her job is to establish that the morning is a real adventure, not ordinary present opening.

## Storyline

A new region has appeared on Professor Oak's map. Unusual energy is spreading through Creekside, and the Pokémon League has invited exactly one new Trainer to investigate. Before Luca can leave, he must register, take the Trainer Oath, and prepare his equipment.

## Interaction and challenge

1. Ariel dramatically confirms Luca's identity.
2. Luca chooses his on-screen partner avatar.
3. Ariel administers a short Trainer Oath:
   - protect Pokémon
   - help friends
   - play fairly
   - never give up
4. Luca completes a quick equipment test:
   - place one card in a sleeve
   - place the sleeved card in a top loader
   - place it in the deck box
5. Adult confirms completion.

## Reward earned

**Open now:**

- Blue deck box
- Red Pokémon card sleeves
- Cardboard Gold top loaders
- Ultra Pro One-Touch Edge case
- Mega Gallade ex as Luca's first recruited teammate

**Earn now, open later during the celebration:**

- Journey Together booster pack

**Quest item:**

- Ranger Code Record Card with four blank numbered spaces
- Physical Code Fragment 1, prepared privately by Patrick

## App update

- Trainer profile created
- Team count becomes 1
- Equipment inventory appears
- Code Fragment Slot 1 becomes recorded, but the app does not display or store the number
- Chapter badge: Trainer License

## Transition

A tiny Fairy-type energy signal appears in the backyard. The app warns that the signal is moving quickly and may need help from the youngest Gym Leader in Creekside.

# Chapter 3: Pokémon Center Emergency

## Location

Home Base porch, patio, or indoor room.

## Character

**Polly: Nurse Joy**

Polly can act warmly but fully in character. A pink apron, headband, or simple nurse accessory is enough.

## Storyline

Three Pokémon arrived at the Center after encountering unstable Mega Energy. Luca must diagnose each one and choose the right treatment before Professor Oak will allow him to continue.

## Interaction and challenge

Prepare three laminated patient cards and three matching treatment cards.

Suggested matches:

- Tired Pikachu → Oran Berry
- Overheated Charmander → Water and rest
- Frightened Eevee → Comfort and a quiet place

Luca must:

1. inspect each patient
2. match the correct treatment
3. carry one “medicine” Poké Ball through a short path without dropping it
4. report back to Nurse Joy

The app may provide one hint after an incorrect match. There is no hard failure.

## Reward earned

**Open now:**

- Pokémon: Meet the Crew reader
- Pokémon mini portfolio with included booster
- Scream Tail ex

**Earn now, open later:**

- The booster included with the mini portfolio

**Quest item:**

- Physical Code Fragment 3

## App update

- Field Pokédex/portfolio unlocked
- Care Badge earned
- Code Fragment Slot 3 recorded

## Transition

Nurse Joy receives an urgent transmission: Professor Oak has detected Pokémon objects beneath the surface of his Water Research Preserve. Luca must report to Professor Oak's Lab at 10:15.

# Chapter 4: Professor Oak's Water Research Lab

## Location

Professor Oak's Lab, pool and pool deck.

## Characters

- **Bruce: Professor Oak**
- **Monica: Professor Monica, Water Research Professor and Oak's Research Partner**

Both are willing to dress up and participate actively.

## Entrance beat

Bruce is facing the pool, taking notes or using binoculars. He quietly says, “Shhh... I think there's a rare Pokémon in the preserve.” He then notices Luca and recognizes him as the new Trainer.

## Storyline

Professor Oak's instruments detected four research capsules in the preserve. One contains the final Ranger Code fragment. Another contains a Sky Fragment producing Mega Energy. Team Rocket may be searching for it.

## Pool challenge

Prepare four clearly numbered, double-sealed or vacuum-sealed research capsules. They may float or rest in a safe shallow area.

Recommended contents:

1. Physical Code Fragment 4
2. One Chaos Rising booster pack
3. One water-themed Pokémon mini tin
4. A laminated or 3D-printed Sky Fragment/Mega Stone prop

Challenge flow:

1. Monica shows Luca a diagram of the four capsule symbols.
2. Luca enters the pool or uses a skimmer, depending on preference.
3. He retrieves all four capsules.
4. Bruce asks him to identify which capsule contains the unstable energy based on its symbol.
5. Luca places the Sky Fragment in a research tray.
6. Adult confirms completion.

Fallback: all capsules can be retrieved with a skimmer from the deck.

## Reward earned

**Open now on the pool deck:**

- First Partner Illustration Collection Series 2
- Pokémon: A New Journey reader
- Pokémon Adventures Collector's Edition 02
- Water-themed mini tin retrieved from the pool

The First Partner box may be opened enough to see the promo cards and sticker sheet. All booster packs remain sealed until the celebration.

**Earn now, open later:**

- Chaos Rising booster retrieved from the pool
- Two boosters inside the First Partner collection
- Packs contained in the mini tin

**Quest item:**

- Physical Code Fragment 4
- Sky Fragment

## App update

- Four code slots show as complete, without numbers
- First Partner research entry added
- Water Research Badge earned
- Sky Fragment added to quest inventory

## Transition

The app glitches with a Team Rocket transmission. Mike has intercepted Oak's data and is guarding the route at Team Rocket Base. Bruce tells Luca to recover the stolen Ranger Dispatch and protect the Sky Fragment.

# Chapter 5: Team Rocket Basketball Base

## Location

Team Rocket Base, basketball hoop.

## Character

**Mike: Team Rocket Enforcer**

Mike should lean into being physically imposing and theatrical. A black shirt with a red R, sunglasses, hat, or gloves is sufficient.

## Storyline

Team Rocket stole the Ranger Dispatch that explains what the four fragments unlock. Mike challenges Luca to break through Team Rocket's basketball defense.

## Interaction and challenge

Use a lowered rim if helpful. The challenge uses cumulative success so Luca cannot get stuck.

Round 1: **Break the Shield**
- Make one close-range basket.

Round 2: **Overload the Defense**
- Score two baskets within five attempts, or earn three total points:
  - basket = 2 points
  - rim/backboard hit = 1 point

Round 3: **Final Poké Shot**
- Luca chooses one recruited Pokémon card as his battler.
- Mike delivers a dramatic taunt.
- Luca makes one final shot from a comfortable marker.
- After two misses, Mike theatrically “loses control of the defense,” and the marker moves closer.

## Reward earned

- Incineroar ex
- Mega Pyroar ex

**Earn now, open later:**

- Destined Rivals booster pack

**Quest item:**

- Ranger Dispatch stating that the four collected fragments form the entry code for the Secret Ranger Vault

## App update

- Secret Ranger Vault unlocked on the map

## Transition

Mike drops the Dispatch while retreating. The app reveals that Hannah and Noa, two Creekside Rangers currently away on expedition, left a protected cache inside their front entry.

# Chapter 6: Secret Ranger Vault

## Location

Secret Ranger Vault, approved front entry only.

## Characters

Hannah and Noa appear through an app message as **Rangers Hannah and Noa**. They are away physically, which is part of the story.

## Security rule

The actual entry code is never stored in the app, repository, documentation, comments, or assets. Luca uses the four physical fragments collected earlier. An adult accompanies him and may take over if needed.

## Storyline

The Rangers knew Team Rocket was searching for the Sky Fragment, so they secured supplies and research material before leaving on a mission. Only a Trainer who protected all four code fragments can enter.

## Interaction and challenge

1. The app displays four empty code positions labeled Fragment 1 through Fragment 4.
2. Luca reads the physical digits from his Ranger Code Record Card.
3. He enters the code on the real keypad with an adult beside him.
4. Inside the approved entry area, he follows three Ranger symbols to the prepared gift bag.
5. The app asks him to locate the sealed Research File inside the bag but not open it until Professor Oak analyzes it.

# Chapter 7: Victory Road and Champion Battle

## Location

Home Base backyard, trees, patio, and yard-game area.

## Characters

- **Auntie Ariel: Elite Four Wild Card / Victory Road Referee**
- **Patrick: Creekside Region Champion**

## Storyline

Luca must cross Victory Road, defeat Ariel's final test, calm Mega Rayquaza, and then challenge the reigning Champion.

## Stage A: Victory Road obstacle course

Set up four fast stations:

1. **Tall Grass:** weave through cones or streamers
2. **Forest Crossing:** balance along a rope, board, or chalk line
3. **Poké Ball Accuracy:** land three balls in a bucket or yard target
4. **Energy Search:** retrieve three hidden energy tokens from the trees

Ariel narrates dramatically and can invent funny penalties that are harmless, such as a five-second Slowpoke walk.

## Stage B: Rayquaza encounter

The app reveals Mega Rayquaza above Sky Pillar.

Physical challenge options:

- hit a hanging Rayquaza target with a soft Poké Ball
- ring-toss over a Rayquaza marker
- assemble three Sky symbols collected during Victory Road

The challenge ends after one clear success or after Ariel invokes a “Legendary Assist.”

## Stage C: Champion Patrick

Three short rounds:

1. **Knowledge:** choose the best type response from three large printed cards
2. **Skill:** one target toss or yard-game shot
3. **Heart:** name one person or Pokémon who helped him during the adventure

Patrick acknowledges that a Champion needs skill, courage, and friends, then concedes the match.

- Mega Rayquaza model kit

**Earn now, open later:**
