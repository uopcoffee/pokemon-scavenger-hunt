# 17. Logistics Update 4 — Adult-Triggered Mew Surprise

## Release objective

V3.5 makes the Champion ending completely convincing. Hall of Heroes, the
Champion photo, credits, and the final Champion screen finish normally and
remain at rest. Mew cannot appear until an adult deliberately confirms the
hidden Parent Mode action.

## Champion ending

The final Luca-facing screen reads:

> Champion Luca  
> Creekside Region Journey Complete

It has no next button, countdown, hidden chapter preview, Mew icon, unknown
signal, or postgame label. Refresh restores the same ending.

## Adult Mew trigger

1. Let the Champion ending sit for roughly 2–5 minutes, or until Luca has
   accepted that the adventure is complete.
2. Open Parent Mode with the existing five-tap adult gesture.
3. Choose **Trigger Mew Signal**.
4. Review the warning.
5. Choose **Confirm Mew Signal**. Choose **Cancel** to leave the ending
   untouched.

The confirmed action persists immediately and opens the existing visual Mew
signal. A normal tap cannot trigger it. The optional vibration is nonessential;
the silent visual alert is complete. System reduced-motion settings remove the
glitch-line animation.

If Mew is triggered too early, Parent Mode offers **Mew triggered too early —
return to Champion ending**. The adult can settle the scene and trigger it
again later.

## Mew phone ownership

- If Patrick physically guides Luca through the three-marker trail, Polly or
  Auntie Ariel is Phone Captain.
- If another adult physically guides Luca, Patrick may remain Phone Captain.
- The Mew figure remains separate from the Champion Chest.

After Mew, the celebration screen retains the popsicle and Booster Satchel
instructions and adds Hall of Heroes replay. Full reset remains Parent-only.

## Party-day phone protocol

Director View now contains concise checklists for preparation, active phone
handling, and recovery from reloads, locks, wrong scenes, absent participants,
skipped challenges, early gift handoffs, an early Mew trigger, and temporary
internet loss.
