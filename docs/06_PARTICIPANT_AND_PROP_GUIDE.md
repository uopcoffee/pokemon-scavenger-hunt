# 06. Participant and Prop Guide

## General performance direction

- Keep dialogue to 20-45 seconds before action.
- Address Luca as “Trainer Luca.”
- Never correct him harshly or make him repeat a failed physical task for long.
- Use big reactions when he succeeds.
- Hand rewards only after the app's success animation.
- Adults may paraphrase. Exact memorization is unnecessary.

## Auntie Ariel

### Role 1: Pokémon League Recruiter

Opening line:

> “Trainer Luca! The Creekside Pokémon League has reviewed your record. Today, you begin your first official mission.”

### Role 2: Victory Road Referee

Style: loud, hilarious, dramatic, and encouraging.

Useful lines:

> “The Tall Grass has never seen speed like that!”

> “Slowpoke penalty! Five very slow steps!”

> “The League recognizes that as completely legitimate Champion behavior!”

Props:

- whistle
- clipboard
- League badge or jacket
- obstacle-course scorecard

## Nina

Role: Fairy Gym Leader and Pikachu's Helper.

Lines should be one or two words:

- “Luca, look!”
- “Tree!”
- “You did it!”

Props:

- crown, wings, or pink headband
- small basket
- three Fairy Energy Orbs

## Polly

Role: Nurse Joy.

Opening line:

> “Trainer Luca, I'm glad you're here. Three Pokémon need help, and I heard you know how to take care of your team.”

Props:

- pink apron or headband
- three patient cards
- three treatment cards
- one medicine Poké Ball

## Bruce

Role: Professor Oak.

Entrance:

Bruce should be observing the pool and taking notes before acknowledging Luca.

Opening line:

> “Shhh... the preserve is unusually active. Wait. You must be Trainer Luca.”

Return-checkpoint line:

> “You came back with the Sky Fragment and the Rangers' file. Excellent. Let's see how far you've come.”

Props:

- white coat
- wig or Professor-style hair if desired
- clipboard
- binoculars
- research tray
- League Authorization card

## Monica

Role: Lead Research Assistant.

Opening line:

> “Professor, the capsule readings are rising. Trainer Luca, we need all four samples before Team Rocket finds them.”

Props:

- lab coat or field vest
- pool skimmer
- capsule diagram
- towel ready for Luca

## Mike

Role: Team Rocket Enforcer.

Opening line:

> “So you're Professor Oak's new Trainer. That Ranger Dispatch belongs to Team Rocket now.”

Loss line:

> “Impossible! My defense was scientifically unbeatable!”

Props:

- black shirt with red R
- sunglasses
- basketball
- three shooting markers
- reward envelope and Ranger Dispatch

## Patrick

Role: Creekside Region Champion.

Opening line:

> “Trainer Luca, I've watched your entire journey. Skills can win a battle, but Champions also need courage and people they trust. Show me all three.”

Concession:

> “The match is over. Luca, you are the new Champion of the Creekside Region.”

Props:

- Champion jacket, cape, or badge
- printed type-choice cards
- final target game
- Champion Chest

## Pool props

- Four vacuum-sealed or double-sealed capsules
- Large visible capsule numbers
- Physical Code Fragment 4
- Chaos Rising pack
- Water-themed mini tin
- Sky Fragment prop
- Research tray
- Towels
- Pool skimmer fallback

Safety:

- One adult watches Luca in the water continuously.
- No running on the pool deck.
- Capsules must not require diving into a deep area.
- Valuable items should be double-contained.

## Basketball props

- Lowered hoop if needed
- Three ground markers
- One standard basketball or child-sized ball
- Optional soft Poké Ball for the final shot
- Simple cumulative score sign

## Backyard props

- cones or streamers
- rope, chalk line, or low balance beam
- bucket or target
- soft balls
- six energy tokens
- Rayquaza target or rings
- three Mew footprint markers
- popsicle cooler

## Fallbacks

- Pool unavailable: use the skimmer from the deck or float capsules in large tubs.
- Mike unavailable: Ariel performs a Team Rocket takeover using the same hoop.
- Nina loses interest: she hands Luca the basket and the app asks him to help her finish.
- Weather issue: move Fairy search and Pokémon Center indoors; run Victory Road under patio/inside using targets and a hallway path.
- Code issue: accompanying adult enters it while Luca reads the four fragments aloud.
