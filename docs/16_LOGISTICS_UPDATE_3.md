# 16. Logistics Update 3 — Real-World Chapter Operations

## Release objective

V3.4 makes the theatrical relay practical on party day. It preserves the
static architecture, story order, physical challenges, rewards, persistence,
Parent Mode, Mew gating, and protected Ranger information while clarifying
phone ownership, safety, gift handling, flexible timing, and adult duties.

## Chapter operations

- Orientation issues the Trainer Kit immediately, introduces the physical
  Trainer Code Card and pencil, and places the first sealed booster in the
  Save for Celebration container.
- Nina may point, hold the basket, help with one object, lose interest, or skip
  the Fairy scene. Luca cannot fail because of her participation.
- The Oak cue opens before pool approach. The phone is stowed, Luca follows the
  one-line water rule, then exits and dries before the phone returns at the dry
  research table. Record the Wave clue in Slot 4; one Ranger clue remains.
- The Pokémon Center is a sheltered restoration stop for drying, changing,
  bathroom, and water as well as the short care challenge.
- Team Rocket uses Close Basket, Defense Target, and Luca’s chosen Final Poké
  Shot. There is no formal score; distance, hoop height, rim, and backboard are
  forgiving tools, and Luca always wins.
- Ranger Vault details remain configurable. Use the physical card with an
  adult, recover the cache, exit, confirm success outside, open one story item,
  and have the bag carrier take larger packages home or to the Professors.
- Oak return is a 5–8 minute item handoff, one callback, Rayquaza finding,
  authorization, compact reward, and immediate Victory Road departure.
- One private Ariel cue covers Victory Road, the Rayquaza reveal and physical
  encounter, and the Champion summon. The phone returns to Luca for both
  Victory Road and Legendary success. Ariel is Champion Phone Captain.
- Gather everyone who is here for the Champion photo. Individual neighbor
  photos may be taken at their own stations.
